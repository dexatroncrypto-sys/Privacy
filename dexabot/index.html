DEXABOT & Quizbot privacy policy

A short, plain explanation of the data we keep when you use DEXABOT, Quizbot, or the DEXATRON Game Hub on Telegram and Discord.

Effective 15 November 2026 UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018

1. Who we are

This policy is issued by the solo developer behind Dexatron, based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”, “Dexatron”). We are the data controller for the game data described here.

It covers DEXABOT, Quizbot, and the DEXATRON Game Hub on Telegram and Discord. It does not cover DEXATRON VR, which has its own policy, or the data Telegram or Discord hold under their own rules.

2. What we store

We store the information needed to run your game account. That is:

  • Your game profile, including your DEX ID and progress, such as DEX balance, battles, quiz scores, leaderboard standing, store or upgrade unlocks, and account settings
  • Your Telegram or Discord user ID and username, so we can tell your profile apart from other players and sign you back in. These identifiers are personal data under UK GDPR, even though they are not your real name
  • The ID of a group, channel or server where the bot is added, so the bot can run in that community
  • If you play cross-platform PvP, a link between your Telegram and Discord identities so you can be matched and keep the same progress

We use the commands and game messages you send to the bot to update that profile. We do not keep a general archive of your private chats.

3. What we do not store

The bots do not store your real name, email address, or payment details. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Why we use it

We use this data to run the games: accounts, battles, quizzes, leaderboards, the store, and cross-platform play. We also use it to fix problems, keep the service secure, and answer support requests.

Under UK GDPR, we do this to provide the service you asked for (our contract with you) and, where needed, for our legitimate interests in security and running a fair game, or to meet a legal obligation. If you later link optional accounts, you can ask us to stop that linking at any time.

5. Telegram, Discord and hosting

The bots run on Telegram and Discord. Those platforms also process your data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. You can read Telegram’s privacy policy and Discord’s privacy policy.

We use hosting and infrastructure providers to run the bots and store game profiles. Those providers may keep short-lived technical logs (for example, to keep the service up and investigate errors). Some providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we use appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your game profile while you use the bots, and for up to 24 months after your last activity, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Technical logs are kept only for a short time, typically up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to deal with a security issue. When we no longer need the data, we delete it or irreversibly anonymise it.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain uses, or (where it applies) receive a portable copy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

To delete your data, contact us using the details below and tell us you want your DEXABOT or Quizbot data deleted. Please include your Telegram or Discord username, and your DEX ID if you have it. We will action a valid request without undue delay, and in any event within one month.

8. Age limit

These bots are for users aged 13 and over, in line with Telegram and Discord’s rules. They are not for children under 13. If we learn that we have data about someone under 13, we will delete it. Please contact us if you think that has happened.
