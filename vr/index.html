1. Who we are

This policy is issued by the solo developer behind Dexatron, based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”, “Dexatron”). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

This policy applies to DEXATRON VR, our fighting game for Meta Quest headsets, and to support we provide for that game. It does not cover the separate DEXABOT and Quizbot services, which have their own policy, or websites we do not control, including Meta’s services.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you play, we may collect the following:

Meta account and entitlement

  • Your Meta platform user ID
  • Your Meta display name and avatar, if the platform provides them
  • Entitlement information, so we can confirm you own or are licensed to play the game

Gameplay data

  • Match results, stats and leaderboard scores
  • Character unlocks, progression and settings

Multiplayer and matchmaking

  • Session information needed to connect you to a match
  • IP address, used to run matchmaking, keep the service secure and deal with abuse
  • Voice chat, if you turn it on: audio is used live to let you talk to other players. We do not record or store voice chat

Device and performance data

  • Headset model and operating system version
  • Crash logs and performance information such as frame rate, used to find and fix problems

Optional Game Hub or DEXABOT linking

If you choose to link DEXATRON VR to the DEXATRON Game Hub or to DEXABOT on Telegram or Discord, we may store the linked account identifiers and related DEX progress so your profile can carry across those services. That linking is optional.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Meta. We may receive purchase records (for example, what you bought and whether it was fulfilled). We do not receive or store your card or other payment details.

Support

If you email us or message us for help, we will keep the contact details and information you send, including any game or device details you include, so we can reply and resolve the issue.

3. What we do not collect or do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data for ads
  • We do not collect precise location
  • We do not store biometric data, camera passthrough imagery, or room-scan data
  • Hand and head tracking is used live to play the game and is not stored
  • There are no crypto wallets or crypto payments inside the game

4. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • Run the game, including accounts, entitlements, matches and progression
  • Provide multiplayer, matchmaking, leaderboards and, if enabled, live voice chat
  • Unlock and fulfil purchases processed by Meta
  • Link your game profile to the Game Hub or DEXABOT if you ask us to
  • Fix bugs, improve performance and keep the game secure
  • Respond to support requests and meet our legal obligations

5. Lawful bases

Under UK GDPR, we rely on these lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide the game and features you use, including multiplayer and purchases you have made
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the game secure, prevent abuse, understand performance, and improve DEXATRON VR, where those interests are not overridden by your rights
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep records, for example purchase or tax records
  • Consent: where we ask for it, such as optional account linking. You can withdraw consent at any time, which will not affect processing already carried out

6. Who we share data with

We only share data where it is needed to run the game or where the law requires it. Recipients may include:

  • Meta Platforms: the game runs on Meta Quest and uses Meta platform services (including entitlement checks and in-app purchases). Meta processes data under its own policies
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: the companies that host this policy page, and any servers or databases we use to operate online features of the game
  • Crash and performance tools: we may use crash reporting or performance analytics, including tools provided by Meta or a similar provider, to diagnose problems
  • Telegram or Discord: only if you choose to link your game profile to DEXABOT or the Game Hub on those platforms

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or to protect the game, our users or our rights.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above:

  • Account, gameplay and linked profile data: while you use the game, and for up to 24 months after your last activity, unless you ask us to delete it sooner
  • Matchmaking session data and IP addresses: for a short period, typically up to 30 days, unless we need them longer to deal with security or abuse
  • Voice chat: not stored
  • Crash and performance logs: typically up to 12 months
  • Support messages: typically up to 24 months
  • Purchase records: for as long as we are required to keep them, which can be up to six years under UK tax rules

When we no longer need the data, we will delete it or irreversibly anonymise it.

8. International transfers

Some of the providers above, including Meta and our website host, may process data in the United States and other countries outside the United Kingdom. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we do so with appropriate safeguards, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

DEXATRON VR is intended for players aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data, in the circumstances set out in the law
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing
  • Receive a copy of data you provided to us, in a portable format, where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise these rights, use the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity, for example by checking your Meta account or game identifier, before we can act on a request.

12. How to delete your data

You can request deletion of your DEXATRON VR data in either of these ways:

  • Use the in-game data deletion option, once it is available in the game settings
  • Contact us using the details below and tell us you want your DEXATRON VR data deleted. Please include your Meta display name or platform ID if you have it

We will action a valid deletion request without undue delay, and in any event within one month, unless the law requires us to keep certain records (for example, purchase records).

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a change is material, we may also notify you in the game or on the store listing.
